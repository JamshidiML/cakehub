# CakeBazar Customer Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the key customer and seller personas for CakeBazar. The
MVP must serve both sides of the marketplace: people planning celebrations and
sellers who can reliably fulfill custom cake orders.

## Persona 1: Busy Family Organizer

Typical profile:

- Parent or family member planning a birthday or family event
- Often ordering from a phone
- May compare options through Instagram, WhatsApp, and recommendations

Job to be done:

- Find a beautiful, reliable cake without spending days messaging sellers.

Pain points:

- Unsure which seller is trustworthy
- Hard to compare prices and quality
- Afraid the cake will not look like the photo
- Needs date, size, flavor, and delivery clarity

Trust triggers:

- Verified seller badge
- Real customer photos
- Recent reviews
- Clear response time
- Simple order request form

MVP requirements:

- Category browsing
- Strong mobile experience
- Clear seller profile
- Easy custom request
- Visible trust signals

## Persona 2: Couple Planning A Wedding Or Anniversary

Typical profile:

- Bride, groom, partner, or family member planning a high-emotion event
- Has higher expectations for design, timing, and presentation

Job to be done:

- Find a seller who can create a premium cake for a meaningful event.

Pain points:

- High risk if the cake fails
- Needs visual confidence before ordering
- Wants customization and consultation
- May need tasting, delivery, or special handling

Trust triggers:

- Premium portfolio
- Verified experience with wedding or anniversary cakes
- Detailed style categories
- Clear quote process
- High reliability score

MVP requirements:

- Occasion-specific category pages
- Portfolio filtering
- Rich listing details
- Upload reference image in order request

## Persona 3: Last-Minute Buyer

Typical profile:

- Customer who needs a cake quickly for a birthday, office event, or family visit
- More practical than design-sensitive

Job to be done:

- Find an available seller quickly.

Pain points:

- Does not know who can deliver soon
- Messaging multiple sellers wastes time
- May accept simpler designs if timing is reliable

Trust triggers:

- Availability indicator
- Fast response badge
- Same-day or next-day filter
- Clear pickup and delivery area

MVP requirements:

- City filter
- Availability field
- Response time signal
- Simple quote request

## Persona 4: Corporate Buyer

Typical profile:

- HR, office manager, founder, or team lead
- Buys for employee birthdays, team events, launches, and client gifts

Job to be done:

- Order reliable celebration cakes without operational hassle.

Pain points:

- Needs invoices and predictable timing
- Wants consistent quality
- May require multiple deliveries or recurring orders

Trust triggers:

- Professional seller profile
- Clear fulfillment terms
- Reviews from organizations
- Admin or account support

MVP requirements:

- Not core MVP, but capture intent through a corporate inquiry form
- Track demand before building corporate workflows

## Seller Persona 1: Home Baker

Typical profile:

- Skilled independent baker selling through Instagram, WhatsApp, or referrals
- Strong craft but limited business tooling

Job to be done:

- Receive qualified orders from customers who trust their work.

Pain points:

- Customers ask vague questions
- Too much back-and-forth before price clarity
- Hard to build trust outside social media
- Needs control over capacity and location

Trust triggers:

- Verified profile
- Portfolio structure
- Clear order brief
- Review collection
- Reliability score that rewards good work

MVP requirements:

- Seller onboarding
- Listing publishing
- Verification process
- Order request inbox or notification
- Admin support

## Seller Persona 2: Professional Bakery

Typical profile:

- Existing bakery or cake designer with a larger catalog and operations
- May already have customers but wants better discovery

Job to be done:

- Reach high-intent customers and convert them efficiently.

Pain points:

- Existing channels may not explain custom work well
- Needs brand control
- Needs reliable order details
- Wants premium positioning

Trust triggers:

- Rich profile
- Category-specific portfolio
- Higher reliability score
- Better search placement

MVP requirements:

- Profile customization
- Multiple listings
- Admin-approved portfolio
- Lead quality tracking

## Persona Priority

MVP priority:

1. Busy Family Organizer
2. Home Baker
3. Professional Bakery
4. Couple planning wedding or anniversary
5. Last-Minute Buyer
6. Corporate Buyer

The first marketplace wedge should serve family events and verified local sellers
before expanding into corporate and complex event workflows.
