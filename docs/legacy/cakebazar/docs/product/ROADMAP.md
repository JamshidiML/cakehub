# CakeBazar Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap describes how CakeBazar should grow from MVP marketplace to trusted
celebration platform. It is organized as Now, Next, Later, and Future so the
product direction stays readable for founders, designers, engineers, and
investors.

## Now: Marketplace MVP

Timeframe: 0 to 6 weeks

Goal: prove that customers want a trusted way to discover and request custom
cakes from verified sellers.

Build:

- Public homepage with clear marketplace positioning
- Category discovery for birthday, wedding, anniversary, children, family, and
  corporate cakes
- City-focused browsing
- Seller registration
- Manual seller verification workflow
- Seller profile pages
- Cake listing pages
- Custom order request form
- Admin dashboard for seller and listing review
- Basic review and rating model
- Reliability score placeholder

Success criteria:

- 20 verified sellers in one launch city or region
- 100 quality listings
- 50 customer order requests
- 10 successful completed orders
- At least 8/10 average customer satisfaction

## Next: Trust Layer

Timeframe: 6 to 12 weeks

Goal: turn a functional marketplace into a trusted marketplace.

Build:

- Verified seller badge rules
- Reliability score v1
- Seller response time tracking
- Order status updates
- Review verification
- Listing quality checklist
- Seller onboarding improvements
- Customer post-event feedback loop
- Notification templates for seller and customer updates

Success criteria:

- 70% of order requests receive seller response within target SLA
- 80% of verified listings pass quality checklist
- 25 successful completed orders
- Repeat purchase intent above 20%
- Customer trust score improves after seller profile redesign

## Later: Transaction And Operations

Timeframe: 3 to 6 months

Goal: reduce operational friction and make completed orders more predictable.

Build:

- Quote management
- Availability calendar
- Pickup and delivery options
- Deposit or payment integration
- Cancellation and refund policy
- Seller availability controls
- Admin dispute workflow
- Search ranking based on trust and relevance
- Category landing pages for SEO

Success criteria:

- Order completion rate above 70%
- Meaningful share of orders with clear quote before customer confirmation
- Fewer failed or ambiguous requests
- Seller satisfaction above 8/10

## Future: Celebration Platform

Timeframe: 6 to 18 months

Goal: expand beyond cakes only after the cake marketplace has strong evidence.

Potential expansion:

- Corporate celebration ordering
- Supplier marketplace for sellers
- Design inspiration library
- AI-assisted cake design brief
- Seller academy
- Event add-ons
- Multi-city launch playbook
- Celebration memory and re-order experience

Do not start this phase until the marketplace has strong trust, supply, and
repeat purchase signals.

## Strategic Non-Goals

Avoid these until the marketplace works:

- Building a broad e-commerce store
- Competing directly as a restaurant delivery app
- Launching in too many cities at once
- Full automation before operational learning
- AI features without a measurable trust or conversion purpose

## Roadmap Rule

Each phase must earn the next phase through evidence. CakeBazar should not grow
because the idea is exciting; it should grow because customers and sellers prove
the next layer is needed.
