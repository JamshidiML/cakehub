# Purpose

# هدف

This document records all experiments conducted by CakeBazar.

The goal is to systematically validate assumptions, reduce uncertainty, and make decisions based on evidence rather than opinions.

---

هدف این سند ثبت تمام آزمایش‌های انجام‌شده در CakeBazar است.

هدف کاهش عدم قطعیت و تصمیم‌گیری مبتنی بر داده به جای حدس و گمان است.

## EXP-001

Title:

Seller Demand Validation

---

Hypothesis:

Home cake makers need additional customer acquisition channels.

---

Experiment:

Interview 20 sellers.

---

Success Criteria:

At least 70% mention customer acquisition as a major challenge.

---

Status:

Proposed

---

## EXP-002

Title:

Customer Trust Validation

---

Hypothesis:

Trust is more important than price.

---

Experiment:

Interview 20 customers.

Would you pay more for a trusted seller?

---

Success Criteria:

More than 50% answer yes.

---

Status:

Proposed
