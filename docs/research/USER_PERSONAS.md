# Product Design Rule

Every new feature must answer:

Which persona does this help?

If no persona benefits, the feature should not be built.

---

هر قابلیت جدید باید به یک پرسونای مشخص خدمت کند.

اگر هیچ پرسونایی از آن بهره نمی‌برد، نباید ساخته شود.

---

# North Star Insight

Customers are not buying cakes.

They are buying successful celebrations.

Sellers are not selling cakes.

They are building businesses.

Organizations are not buying products.

They are creating employee experiences.

CakeBazar sits at the intersection of all three.
