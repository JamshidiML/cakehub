# Competitor 1

# Instagram

---

## Why Customers Use It

چرا مشتریان استفاده می‌کنند؟

* Large number of sellers
* Visual content
* Familiar environment
* Direct communication

---

## Why Sellers Use It

چرا فروشندگان استفاده می‌کنند؟

* Free access
* Easy content publishing
* Existing audience

---

## Strengths

نقاط قوت

* Huge user base
* Strong visual experience
* Established habits
* Low barrier to entry

---

## Weaknesses

نقاط ضعف

* No verification
* No reliability score
* No order management
* No comparison system
* No guarantees
* No dispute resolution

---

## Strategic Opportunity

فرصت استراتژیک

CakeBazar can provide trust, structure, and reliability where Instagram cannot.

---

# Competitor 2

# WhatsApp

---

## Current Usage

Many sellers manage orders manually through WhatsApp.

---

## Strengths

* Simple communication
* Familiar interface

---

## Weaknesses

* No marketplace
* No reviews
* No reputation system
* No automation
* Difficult scalability

---

## Strategic Opportunity

CakeBazar can automate what WhatsApp cannot.

---

# Competitor 3

# Telegram

---

## Current Usage

Many home businesses sell through Telegram channels.

---

## Strengths

* Community building
* Low cost
* Easy communication

---

## Weaknesses

* Lack of trust mechanisms
* No order tracking
* No quality verification

---

## Strategic Opportunity

CakeBazar can combine marketplace functionality with Telegram communication.

---

# Competitor 4

## Weaknesses

## Strategic Opportunity

CakeBazar can aggregate hundreds of sellers in one platform.

# Strategic Moat

# خندق رقابتی

The strongest moat will not be technology.

The strongest moat will be the combination of:

* Trust
* Community
* Data
* Seller Network
* Celebration History
* AI Intelligence

---

قوی‌ترین مزیت رقابتی CakeBazar فناوری نخواهد بود.

قوی‌ترین مزیت رقابتی ترکیب موارد زیر خواهد بود:

* اعتماد

* داده

* تاریخچه مناسبت‌ها

# Key Strategic Insight

# مهم‌ترین بینش استراتژیک

Customers do not currently have a trusted platform for celebrations.

Most competitors solve only one part of the problem.

CakeBazar's opportunity is to own the entire celebration journey.

---

امروز مشتریان یک پلتفرم قابل اعتماد برای مدیریت مناسبت‌ها ندارند.

بیشتر رقبا تنها بخشی از مشکل را حل می‌کنند.

فرصت اصلی CakeBazar در اختیار گرفتن کل مسیر برگزاری مناسبت‌ها است.

---

# Conclusion

# جمع‌بندی

The primary competitors today are:

1. Instagram
2. WhatsApp
3. Personal Referrals
4. Local Bakeries

The long-term competitors will be:

1. Large Marketplaces
2. Food Delivery Platforms
3. Future Celebration Platforms

The winning strategy is not to become cheaper.

The winning strategy is to become more trusted, more reliable, and more valuable.

---

استراتژی برنده ارزان‌تر بودن نیست.
