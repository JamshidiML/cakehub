# Purpose
# هدف

This document defines the release strategy and product evolution of CakeBazar.

The objective is to ensure focused execution, controlled growth, and successful product-market fit.

---

هدف این سند مشخص کردن مسیر انتشار نسخه‌های مختلف محصول است.

# Product Development Philosophy

Build.

Measure.

Learn.

Improve.

Repeat.

بساز.

Never build Phase 2 before validating Phase 1.

# Release Gates

Before any new version:

Must Validate:

- Customer Demand
- Seller Demand
- Revenue Impact
- Retention Impact
- Operational Readiness

---

قبل از هر نسخه جدید باید اعتبارسنجی شود:

- تقاضای مشتری
- تقاضای فروشنده
- تأثیر مالی
- تأثیر بر نگهداری کاربران
- آمادگی عملیاتی

---

# Things We Will Not Rush

- AI Platform
- Academy
- Corporate Platform
- BNPL
- Supplier Marketplace

---

Reason:

Need marketplace validation first.

---

# Monthly Release Review

Questions:

1. What did we learn?
2. What should we stop building?
3. What created the most value?
4. What created the least value?
5. What is blocking Product-Market Fit?
