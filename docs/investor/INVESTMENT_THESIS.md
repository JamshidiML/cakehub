# Investment Thesis

CakeBazar is not simply a cake marketplace.

CakeBazar is building the infrastructure layer for celebrations.

---

CakeBazar فقط یک مارکت‌پلیس کیک نیست.

# Thesis 1

Large Market

# بازار بزرگ

---

The cake market alone is meaningful.

However, the real opportunity is significantly larger.

---

Future expansion markets include:

* Celebrations
* Gifts
* Corporate Events
* Supplier Networks
* Education
* AI Services
* Financial Services

---

فرصت واقعی فراتر از بازار کیک است.

CakeBazar به سمت کل اکوسیستم مناسبت‌ها حرکت می‌کند.

---

# Thesis 2

Highly Fragmented Market

# بازار بسیار پراکنده

---

The current market consists of:

* Instagram sellers
* Home businesses
* Local bakeries
* Informal networks

---

No dominant digital platform exists.

---

بازار فعلی بسیار پراکنده است و رهبر مشخصی ندارد.

این شرایط فرصت مناسبی برای ساخت یک پلتفرم ملی ایجاد می‌کند.

# Thesis 3

Trust Problem

# مشکل اعتماد

---

Trust is one of the biggest pain points in the market.

Customers worry about:

* Product quality
* Delivery reliability
* Seller credibility

---

CakeBazar is designed around trust.

---

Core trust systems:

* Seller Verification
* Reliability Scores
* Reviews
* Celebration Guarantee

---

اعتماد یکی از بزرگ‌ترین مشکلات بازار است و هسته اصلی استراتژی CakeBazar محسوب می‌شود.

Network Effects

# اثر شبکه‌ای

---

As more sellers join:

Customer value increases.

As more customers join:

Seller value increases.

---

As more transactions occur:

Trust data improves.

---

As trust improves:

Marketplace value increases.

---

هرچه تعداد کاربران بیشتر شود، ارزش پلتفرم بیشتر می‌شود.
